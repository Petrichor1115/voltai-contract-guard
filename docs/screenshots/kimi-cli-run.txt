  ## 3. 修订措辞（Redline）

  **① 第 8 条 责任上限（P0）**
  替换句："Supplier's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the amounts paid or payable by Distributor under the affected order in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit or loss of goodwill. These limitations shall not apply to (a) death or personal injury caused by negligence, (b) fraud or wilful misconduct, or (c) Supplier's IP indemnity under Clause 11, which shall be capped at two hundred percent (200%) of the contract value."
  谈判底线：必须存在总额上限且排除间接损失；IP 赔偿必须单独设上限。
  可让步项：上限 100% → 200%；人身/欺诈例外无条件接受。

  **② 第 9 条 迟延罚则（P0）**
  替换句："If Supplier fails to deliver on the agreed date for reasons not attributable to Distributor, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages of zero point five percent (0.5%) of the value of the delayed Products per week of delay, up to a maximum of five percent (5%) of that value, which shall be Distributor's sole remedy for late delivery. An equivalent penalty of zero point five percent (0.5%) per week, capped at five percent (5%), shall apply to Distributor for late payment or late forecast."
  谈判底线：周费率 ≤0.5%、总额上限 ≤10%、必须双向对等。
  可让步项：上限 5% → 10%；周费率 0.5% → 1%。

  **③ 第 11 条 知识产权（P0）**
  替换句："All intellectual property rights in the Products, including software, firmware and documentation, remain vested in Supplier. Supplier grants Distributor a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to use the Products and Supplier's marks solely for resale and promotion in the Territory during the term. Customisation developed and paid for by Distributor shall be owned by Distributor, excluding Supplier's background IP. Supplier shall defend Distributor against third-party claims that the unmodified Products infringe an IP right and indemnify damages finally awarded, provided Distributor gives prompt notice, grants Supplier sole control of the defence and does not modify the Products."
  谈判底线：背景 IP 与固件必须留在供应商；赔偿范围限「未修改产品」并含抗辩控制权。
  可让步项：定制成果归经销商；商标许可可扩至售后展示。

  **④ 第 5 条 付款与账期（P0）**
  替换句："Distributor shall pay each invoice within sixty (60) days after the invoice date. Overdue amounts shall bear interest at one percent (1%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower. Supplier may suspend further deliveries and revoke exclusivity if any invoice remains unpaid for more than thirty (30) days after its due date."
  谈判底线：账期 ≤90 天；逾期利息与暂停交付权两项必须保留至少一项（建议两项全留）。
  可让步项：60 天 → 90 天；利息 1%/月 → 0.5%/月。

  **⑤ 第 10 条 期限与终止（P0）**
  替换句："The Agreement continues for three (3) years and renews annually unless either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice before the end of the then-current term. Either party may terminate for convenience upon one hundred eighty (180) days' notice, or immediately on the other's insolvency or on a material breach remaining uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice. On termination for convenience by Distributor, Distributor shall purchase all ordered but undelivered Products and all finished goods held for it at the price paid, and shall pay all invoices due."
  谈判底线：双方对等便利终止 + 通知期 ≥120 天 + 终止后库存回购。
  可让步项：180 天 → 120 天；回购限于成品与 12 个月内采购的物料。

  **⑥ 第 4 条 价格与汇率（P0）**
  替换句："Prices are stated in EUR. Prices may be adjusted once every twelve (12) months by the change in the producer price index for battery cells, and by the full amount of any increase in cell or freight costs exceeding three percent (3%). If the EUR/USD rate moves by more than five percent (5%) against the rate on the Effective Date, either party may require a price review within thirty (30) days."
  谈判底线：至少保留汇率联动（±5% 触发复核）+ 年度调价机制；固定 5 年不可接受。
  可让步项：调价幅度封顶 10%/年；复核周期 12 → 6 个月。
