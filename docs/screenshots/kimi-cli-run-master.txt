  ## C. 修订措辞（Redline，P0 优先，6 条）

  > 其余 P0/P1 的可替换句已写入上表"修改建议"列；以下 6 条为谈判桌上必须先落的锚点。

  **C1｜第 9 条 迟延违约金（对应 R2）**

  > 9.1 If Supplier fails to deliver by the agreed delivery date for reasons not attributable to Distributor or to a Force Majeure Event, Supplier shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the value of the delayed Products per completed week of delay, up to an aggregate maximum of 5% of the value of the delayed Products.
  > 9.2 If the delay exceeds eight (8) weeks, Distributor may cancel the affected order and recover any prepayment, without prejudice to liquidated damages already accrued.
  > 9.3 Such liquidated damages are the sole remedy for delay. A late-payment charge of 0.05% per day, capped at 5% of the overdue amount, shall apply to Distributor's overdue invoices.

  *谈判底线*：必须有上限，且上限 ≤ 10% 订单额；费率 ≤ 1%/周；对方逾期付款须有对应利息。
  *可让步项*：费率 0.5%→1%/周；上限 5%→10%；给 2 周宽限期；取消权门槛 8 周→10 周。

  **C2｜第 8 条 责任限制（对应 R1）**

  > 8.1 Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of business or loss of goodwill.
  > 8.2 Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed 100% of the amounts paid or payable by Distributor under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
  > 8.3 The limitations in Clauses 8.1 and 8.2 shall not apply to death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud or wilful misconduct, breach of Clause 12, infringement of the other party's intellectual property, or the indemnity in Clause 11.
  > 8.4 Supplier shall not be liable for defects or loss caused by Distributor's modification, misuse, improper storage or installation, or failure to maintain the Products in accordance with Supplier's instructions.

  *谈判底线*：必须存在总额上限——上限 100%–200% 的 12 个月付款额之间的任何数字都可接受；间接损失必须双向排除。
  *可让步项*：上限 100%→200%；按单次索赔上限＋年度累计上限双层结构；例外项删减至人身伤害、欺诈、IP 三项。

  **C3｜第 5 条 付款（对应 R3）**

  > 5.1 Distributor shall pay each invoice within sixty (60) days of the invoice date, by irrevocable wire transfer in USD.
  > 5.2 Overdue amounts shall bear interest at 1% per month from the due date until paid.
  > 5.3 If any amount remains unpaid fifteen (15) days after the due date, Supplier may by written notice suspend further deliveries and cancel outstanding orders without liability, and all outstanding invoices shall become immediately due.
  > 5.4 Payment shall not be conditional on resale, installation or acceptance by end customers.

  *谈判底线*：账期 ≤ 90 天；利息与停供权两项不可同时被删（这是唯一的履约杠杆）。
  *可让步项*：60 天→90 天；利息 1%/月→0.5%/月；停供前给 15 天书面催告期。

This indemnity is subject to Clause 8 and excludes claims arising from Distributor's modifications, combinations or use outside the specifications.

  *谈判底线*：产品/固件/软件 IP 必须留在我方；赔偿范围必须收窄到 IP 侵权并受第 8 条上限约束。
  *可让步项*：就地为对方开发的非核心交付物可共有或授予排他使用许可；抗辩控制权可改为共同抗辩。

  **C5｜第 10 条 期限与终止（对应 R5、R7）**

  > 10.1 The Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for three (3) years, renewing automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives ninety (90) days' notice of non-renewal.
  > 10.2 Either party may terminate for convenience on twelve (12) months' written notice; orders accepted before the notice date shall be fulfilled and paid.
  > 10.3 Either party may terminate immediately if the other party (a) commits a material breach and fails to cure it within thirty (30) days of written notice, (b) becomes insolvent, or (c) in Supplier's case, fails to meet the annual minimum purchase volume in Annex II for two consecutive contract years.
  > 10.4 On termination, Distributor shall pay for all Products delivered and all work in progress ordered by Distributor, and Supplier shall repurchase saleable Products in Distributor's stock at the price paid, net of amounts owed to Supplier.

  *谈判底线*：终止权必须双向对等；我方必须有业绩型终止权；终止时在制订单与库存必须有归属规则。

  **C6｜第 6 条 交付与风险（对应 R6）**

  > 6.1 Delivery shall be FCA Supplier's factory (Incoterms 2020). Supplier shall load the Products onto the carrier nominated by Distributor, and risk of loss or damage shall pass to Distributor upon loading.
