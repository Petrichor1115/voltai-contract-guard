# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT (Mock A — high risk)

**Contract No.:** WT-DIST-2026-014
**Supplier:** Voltai Energy Storage GmbH (Germany)
**Distributor:** Nordwind Energy Solutions B.V. (Netherlands)
**Effective Date:** 1 November 2026

## 1. Definitions

"Products" means the residential and commercial battery energy storage systems listed in Annex I. "Territory" means the Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg.

## 2. Appointment

Supplier appoints Distributor as its exclusive distributor in the Territory. Distributor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to promote the Products. No minimum purchase commitment is agreed by either party.

## 3. Orders and Acceptance

Distributor shall issue purchase orders at least 90 days before the requested delivery date. Supplier shall confirm or reject an order within 10 business days. Products shall be deemed accepted by Distributor seven (7) days after delivery, unless Distributor submits a written objection describing a material defect.

## 4. Price and Taxes

Prices are stated in USD and are fixed for the entire term. All customs duties, VAT, withholding taxes and other levies in the Territory shall be borne by Distributor. No price adjustment mechanism applies.

## 5. Payment

Distributor shall pay each invoice within one hundred twenty (120) days after the invoice date. No interest shall accrue on late payments. Supplier shall not suspend deliveries for late payment.

## 6. Delivery and Risk

Supplier shall deliver the Products DAP Rotterdam. Risk of loss shall pass to Distributor upon loading at the Supplier's factory. Title shall pass upon full payment.

## 7. Warranty

Supplier warrants that the Products conform to the specifications for twelve (12) months from the date of shipment from the factory. Supplier's sole obligation is to repair defective Products at its factory. No field service, replacement or response time commitment is provided.

## 8. Limitation of Liability

Supplier shall be liable for all losses, damages, costs and expenses of any kind incurred by Distributor arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including indirect and consequential losses, without any limitation of amount.

## 9. Delay Penalties

If Supplier fails to deliver on the agreed date, Supplier shall pay Distributor a penalty of thirty percent (30%) of the total order value per week of delay, without any cap. No equivalent penalty applies to Distributor for late payment or late forecasting.

## 10. Term and Termination

The Agreement enters into force on the Effective Date and continues for five (5) years. Distributor may terminate this Agreement at any time for convenience upon thirty (30) days' notice. Supplier may terminate only if Distributor becomes insolvent or commits a material breach that remains uncured for ninety (90) days.

## 11. Intellectual Property

All intellectual property rights in any deliverables, customization, software or documentation created under this Agreement shall vest exclusively in Distributor. Supplier shall indemnify Distributor against any third-party claim relating to the Products.

## 12. Confidentiality

Distributor's confidential information shall be kept confidential by Supplier for an unlimited period. Supplier's confidential information may be disclosed by Distributor to its affiliates, resellers and service providers without restriction.

## 13. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delay caused by force majeure. Supply chain disruptions, component shortages and freight delays shall not constitute force majeure for Supplier. If force majeure continues for thirty (30) days, Distributor may terminate immediately without compensation.

## 14. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the Netherlands. Any dispute shall be finally settled by arbitration in Amsterdam under the rules of the Netherlands Arbitration Institute, in the Dutch language.

## 15. Compliance

Supplier warrants that it complies with all applicable sanctions, export control and battery regulations in all jurisdictions worldwide. Distributor may terminate immediately if Supplier fails to provide any compliance certificate requested by Distributor.

## 16. Miscellaneous

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions. Distributor may assign this Agreement to any affiliate without Supplier's consent. Supplier may not assign this Agreement without Distributor's prior written consent.
