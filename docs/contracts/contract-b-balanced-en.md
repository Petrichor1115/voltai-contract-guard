# DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT (Mock B — balanced)

**Contract No.:** WT-DIST-2026-021
**Supplier:** Voltai Energy Storage GmbH (Germany)
**Distributor:** Ibervolt Suministros S.L. (Spain)
**Effective Date:** 1 December 2026

## 1. Appointment

Supplier appoints Distributor as a non-exclusive distributor for Spain and Portugal. Distributor shall purchase a minimum of 40 MWh per contract year.

## 2. Forecast and Orders

Distributor shall provide a rolling six-month forecast on the first business day of each month. Forecasts are non-binding except for the first 60 days. Orders are binding upon written confirmation by Supplier.

## 3. Price and Currency

Prices are stated in EUR, DAP Valencia. Either party may request a price review if the EUR/USD rate moves more than 7% from the rate on the Effective Date; the review shall be completed within 15 days. VAT and import duties in the Territory are borne by Distributor.

## 4. Payment

Payment shall be made within thirty (30) days after the invoice date by bank transfer. Late payments accrue interest at 2% per annum above EURIBOR. Supplier may suspend further deliveries if an undisputed invoice remains unpaid for 45 days after written notice.

## 5. Delivery and Acceptance

Supplier shall deliver DAP Valencia. Risk passes to Distributor upon delivery at the named place. Distributor shall inspect the Products within thirty (30) days of delivery and may reject non-conforming Products with a written defect report.

## 6. Warranty

Supplier warrants the Products for twenty-four (24) months from the date of delivery against defects in materials and workmanship. Supplier shall repair or replace defective Products at its option. Field service response time is 5 business days for P1 faults.

## 7. Limitation of Liability

Each party's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the total amounts paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. This limitation does not apply to fraud, wilful misconduct, death or personal injury, or breach of confidentiality.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience upon ninety (90) days' written notice. Termination for material breach requires thirty (30) days' cure period. On termination, Supplier shall repurchase unused Products at the original price less freight costs.

## 9. Intellectual Property

Supplier retains all intellectual property in the Products and embedded software. Distributor may use Supplier's trademarks solely for promoting the Products in the Territory. Each party shall indemnify the other against third-party claims caused by its own products or conduct.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other's confidential information for five (5) years after termination, with standard exceptions for public domain information and legally required disclosures.

## 11. Force Majeure

Force majeure includes events beyond a party's reasonable control. The affected party shall notify the other within 14 days. If the event continues for 90 days, either party may terminate the affected orders without liability.

## 12. Governing Law and Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Germany. Disputes shall be finally settled by arbitration under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce (ICC), seat Hamburg, language English.

## 13. Compliance

Each party shall comply with applicable export control, sanctions and anti-bribery laws, including the EU Dual-Use Regulation and the EU Batteries Regulation.

## 14. Assignment

Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except to an affiliate with notice.
